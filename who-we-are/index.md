# Who we are

Universal Wings is a trade-focused team based in Nepal. We are connectors, coordinators, and problem-solvers for businesses involved in import and export.

## Our purpose

To create dependable pathways for products and businesses to move between Nepal and the world.

## Our vision

To be a trusted, responsible, and practical partner for cross-border trade involving Nepal.

## Our mission

We help businesses trade more confidently by combining market connections, careful coordination, responsive communication, and respect for every requirement that makes a shipment successful.

## How we work

1. **Listen:** We understand your product, destination, quantity, budget, timeline, and expectations.
2. **Assess:** We review the opportunity and identify key commercial, logistical, and documentation considerations.
3. **Coordinate:** We connect the relevant suppliers, buyers, agents, carriers, and service providers.
4. **Communicate:** We share clear updates and flag issues early.
5. **Deliver:** We stay focused on the agreed outcome and the next practical step.

## Our promise

We will be clear about what we know, what needs confirmation, and what can affect your trade plan. We aim to earn trust through consistent work—not just through words.
