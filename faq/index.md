# Frequently asked questions

## What does Universal Wings import and export?

We work across a range of product categories. Each opportunity is assessed based on product, origin, destination, specifications, compliance requirements, and commercial viability.

## Can you source a product for my business?

Yes. Share your product brief, target quantity, quality expectations, budget, and destination. We can then discuss suitable sourcing and coordination options.

## Do you work with Nepalese producers and exporters?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from producers and businesses in Nepal that want to explore buyers, distributors, or markets outside Nepal.

## Can you arrange customs clearance?

We can help coordinate the import or export process and connect relevant service providers. The exact scope is confirmed for each engagement. Regulatory and customs requirements should be verified with the appropriate authorities or licensed professionals.

## How long does an import or export shipment take?

Timing depends on product availability, route, documentation, approvals, carrier schedules, customs, and delivery location. We provide an estimate after reviewing the details.

## What information should I send with my enquiry?

Product description, quantity, origin, destination, specifications, budget, timeline, and any special requirements are the most useful starting points.

## Do you handle small orders?

We review enquiries individually. Tell us your expected quantity and requirements so we can discuss the most practical approach.
