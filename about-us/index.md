# About Universal Wings

## Our company

Registered as **Universal Wings Marketing Center Pvt. Ltd.**, with Registration No. **124234/070/71** and PAN / VAT No. **602392408**, we are an export and import agency and a complete export / import hub.

We bring branded and quality products to customers’ doorsteps while helping connect customers, suppliers, and business partners across markets.

## Our product range

We export and import a wide range of products, including:

- Beverages and liquors
- Furniture and electrical appliances
- Health and beauty products
- Watches and luxury goods
- Food products and frozen food items
- Poultry products
- Garments and raw materials
- Ayurvedic products and handicrafts
- Stationery
- Construction materials and equipment
- Lubricants

Our product range continues to grow in response to the needs of our customers and partners.

## Our goal

Our goal is to provide quality and branded products to our customers and partners worldwide. We aim to make sourcing and international trade more accessible, dependable, and convenient through clear communication and coordinated service.

**Universal Wings Marketing Center Pvt. Ltd. is a part of Universal Wings Investment.**

We work at the intersection of suppliers, buyers, products, documentation, and logistics. Our role is to turn complex trade requirements into a process that is easier to understand and manage.

## Our approach

We believe good trade is built on good information. That means understanding the product, confirming expectations, communicating clearly, and keeping each part of the process aligned.

Our work is guided by four principles:

- **Clarity:** We explain requirements, responsibilities, and next steps in plain language.
- **Reliability:** We follow through on agreed actions and keep stakeholders informed.
- **Responsibility:** We encourage compliant, ethical, and sustainable business practices.
- **Relationships:** We focus on trust that lasts beyond a single transaction.

## Nepal, connected globally

Nepal has a growing community of producers, entrepreneurs, manufacturers, retailers, and service providers. At the same time, businesses in Nepal need dependable access to international products and suppliers.

Universal Wings helps create those connections. We combine local understanding with an international outlook to support trade that benefits businesses on both sides.

## A practical partner for trade

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all trade solutions. We listen to your requirements, identify the important details, and help build a route that fits your product, market, and timeline.

If you are considering a new supplier, product, buyer, or market, we would be glad to hear from you.
