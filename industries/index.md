# Industries we serve

Our trade support can be adapted to businesses across different sectors, including:

- Retail and wholesale
- Hospitality and food service
- Manufacturing and production
- Agriculture and agro-processing
- Construction and infrastructure
- Consumer goods
- Small and growing enterprises
- Producers and craft businesses

Different industries have different requirements. We take time to understand the product, route, documents, and expectations before recommending next steps.
