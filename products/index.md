# Products and sourcing

Universal Wings works with product requirements rather than limiting every opportunity to a fixed catalogue. Availability, origin, specifications, and trade eligibility are confirmed case by case.

## Product categories

Depending on your requirement, we may help explore opportunities involving:

- Agricultural and natural products
- Food and beverage products
- Herbs, spices, and specialty products
- Handicrafts and cultural products
- Textiles, garments, and lifestyle goods
- Construction and industrial supplies
- Machinery, tools, and equipment
- Consumer and business products

If you have a product not listed here, contact us. The best starting point is a clear description of what you need or what you want to sell.

## Share a product brief

Please include:

- Product name and specifications
- Quantity or expected order volume
- Origin or destination
- Quality, packaging, or certification requirements
- Target budget or price range
- Preferred delivery timeline
