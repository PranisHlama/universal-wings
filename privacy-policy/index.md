# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** [Add date]

Universal Wings respects your privacy. This policy explains how we may use information you provide through this website.

## Information we may receive

We may receive your name, business name, email address, phone number, location, product requirements, and any other information you choose to include in an enquiry.

## How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, assess trade opportunities, provide requested services, communicate about an engagement, and improve our website and service.

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers or partners only when needed to respond to your request or deliver an agreed service, subject to appropriate safeguards.

## Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, legal obligations, or legitimate business records. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.
