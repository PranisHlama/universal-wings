# What we do

Universal Wings provides practical support for businesses importing into Nepal, exporting from Nepal, or exploring a new trade opportunity.

## Import services

We help coordinate the journey from an international supplier to your business in Nepal.

### Import categories

Our import categories include:

- Food and beverages
- Furniture
- Garments
- Cosmetics
- Electronics
- Toys

- Product and supplier sourcing
- Requirement and specification coordination
- Supplier communication and quotation support
- Purchase and shipment coordination
- Import documentation support
- Freight and logistics coordination
- Customs and clearance coordination with relevant professionals
- Delivery planning and status updates

## Export services

We support Nepalese businesses that want to present their products to buyers and markets outside Nepal.

### Export categories

Our export categories include:

- Thanka
- Paper craft
- Carpets
- Woolen products
- Food and beverages
- Furniture
- Pashmina

- Export opportunity assessment
- Buyer and market introduction support
- Product information and quotation coordination
- Order and shipment planning
- Export documentation coordination
- Freight and logistics coordination
- Communication between producers, buyers, and service providers

## Product sourcing

When you need a particular product or supplier, we help define the requirement and explore suitable options. A useful sourcing brief may include product specifications, target quantity, preferred origin, quality expectations, delivery location, target price, and required date.

## Trade coordination

Trade often depends on many parties working together. We help keep information moving between buyers, sellers, freight providers, customs representatives, and other relevant partners.

## Important note

Services and responsibilities are confirmed for each engagement. Import and export requirements can vary by product, origin, destination, and current regulation. Final compliance, licensing, tax, customs, and product approvals should be confirmed with the appropriate authorities and qualified professionals.
