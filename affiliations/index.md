## Building stronger trade connections

These affiliations reflect our commitment to participating in Nepal’s commercial community and developing meaningful relationships with businesses, institutions, suppliers, buyers, and trade partners.

Through these networks, we continue to support responsible trade, business cooperation, and opportunities for Nepalese products and international markets.
